================================================================================
WHEELS STRATEGIC BRIEF — 05: TRUST & SAFETY
How to build a platform that mechanics trust and shops respect.
================================================================================

THE FUNDAMENTAL TENSION
------------------------
Mechanics want to speak freely about bad shops. Shops want to defend 
themselves against false claims. Both are valid. Both will kill your 
platform if you get the balance wrong.

ARCHITECTURE: TRI-STATE REVIEW SYSTEM
--------------------------------------
Every review has three verification states:

1. ANONYMOUS (Green badge: "Community Voice")
   - Anyone can post
   - Lowest weight in algorithms
   - No identity verification required
   - Subject to community moderation (upvote/downvote)
   - Why keep it: Some mechanics fear retaliation. Anonymous voices 
     surface problems that verified voices won't.

2. VERIFIED-EMPLOYEE (Orange badge: "Verified Pro")
   - Proven current or former employee
   - Highest weight in algorithms
   - Verified via: pay stub, W-2, union membership, or employer confirmation
   - Can be anonymous to the public but verified to the platform
   - Why it matters: This is your gold standard. 1 verified review = 
     10 anonymous reviews in trust value.

3. VERIFIED-CUSTOMER (Blue badge: "Verified Customer")
   - Proven service customer
   - Medium weight in algorithms
   - Verified via: receipt, appointment confirmation, or shop confirmation
   - Why it matters: Customers see different things than employees. 
     Both perspectives matter.

ANTI-GAMING MEASURES
---------------------

1. REVIEW VELOCITY DETECTION
   Flag shops that get 5+ reviews in 24 hours. Require manual review 
   before publishing. Detects coordinated fake review campaigns.

2. LINGUISTIC FINGERPRINTING
   AI analysis of writing style. If 10 "different" reviews use the same 
   phrases, sentence structure, and emoji patterns, flag as coordinated.

3. EMPLOYER CONFIRMATION ESCROW
   Reviews held 48 hours. Shop gets notification: "A verified employee 
   review is pending. You may respond before publish." Shop can dispute 
   facts (not opinions). Disputes go to human moderation.

4. DEFAMATION INSURANCE (Optional)
   Shops pay $19/mo for legal protection against demonstrably false 
   reviews. If a review is proven false in court, insurance covers 
   legal costs up to $10K. You partner with a legal tech firm (e.g., 
   LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer).

5. MENTAL HEALTH FLAGGING
   AI sentiment analysis detects burnout, depression, suicidal ideation 
   in reviews. Auto-offer resources:
     - 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
     - Union EAP programs
     - Local mental health resources
   
   Do NOT sell this feature. Do NOT use this data for targeting. Just 
   be human. Mechanics have one of the highest suicide rates of any 
   trade. You have a responsibility.

6. RETALIATION PROTECTION
   If a mechanic leaves a negative review and is fired within 90 days, 
   flag the shop for investigation. Offer the mechanic legal referral 
   (whistleblower protections may apply). This is your trust moat.

7. ZERO-KNOWLEDGE EMPLOYMENT VERIFICATION
   Cryptographic proof that a mechanic worked at a shop without revealing 
   identity, dates, or any other details. Shop verifies employment 
   without seeing the mechanic's profile. Ultimate privacy. Ultimate 
   trust. No employment database to hack.

COMMUNITY MODERATION
---------------------
- Trusted mechanic reviewers (100+ helpful reviews) get moderation 
  privileges (flag, not delete)
- Shop owners can respond to reviews but cannot delete them
- 3-strike system for fake reviews: warning → suspension → permanent ban
- Public transparency report: monthly stats on reviews removed, 
  accounts banned, appeals granted
