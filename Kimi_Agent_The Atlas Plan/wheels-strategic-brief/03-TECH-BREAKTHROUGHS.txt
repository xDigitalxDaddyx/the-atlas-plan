================================================================================
WHEELS STRATEGIC BRIEF — 03: TECH BREAKTHROUGHS
State-of-the-art technologies that could make WHEELS uncopyable in 2026.
================================================================================

PRODUCTION READY (Use these now)
---------------------------------

1. AGENTIC AI
   Autonomous agents that negotiate job offers on behalf of mechanics.
   
   Example: "My agent won't let me work for less than $45/hr with tool 
   allowance, 2 weeks PTO, and ASE reimbursement." The agent fields offers, 
   asks clarifying questions, and only surfaces deals that meet criteria.
   
   Stack: OpenAI Assistants API or LangChain agents. Persistent memory 
   of mechanic preferences. Shop-facing dashboard shows agent negotiations.
   
   Why this is huge: It removes the awkwardness of salary negotiation 
   for mechanics who hate asking for more money. The agent is the bad guy.

2. PASSKEYS / WEBAUTHN
   Replace Firebase phone auth with cryptographic passkeys. Biometric login 
   (Face ID, fingerprint). No SMS interception. No number changes. No 
   password resets. True persistent identity.
   
   Stack: Native Passkeys on iOS 16+ / Android 14+. Fallback to password 
   manager integration (1Password, Bitwarden).
   
   Why this matters: Phone auth is great for emerging markets, terrible 
   for professional identity. Mechanics change numbers. Shops have landlines.
   Passkeys are the future of auth.

3. MULTIMODAL AI (GPT-4V / CLAUDE 3)
   Beyond text — analyze photos, videos, documents.
   
   Applications:
     - Shop photo analysis (safety, cleanliness, organization)
     - Pay stub OCR and verification
     - Tool identification from photos
     - Receipt parsing for tool inventory
   
   Stack: OpenAI GPT-4V API or Anthropic Claude 3. Fine-tune on shop 
   photos for domain-specific accuracy.

4. WHISPER API (VOICE-TO-TEXT)
   Open-source speech recognition. Handles accents, background noise, 
   technical jargon. Perfect for shop-floor review dictation.
   
   Stack: OpenAI Whisper API or self-hosted Whisper Large v3. Real-time 
   streaming for live dictation.

RESEARCH → PRODUCTION (6-12 month horizon)
-------------------------------------------

5. FEDERATED LEARNING
   Train salary prediction models across user devices without centralizing 
   sensitive pay data. Each phone trains locally on its own data. Only 
   model updates (not raw data) are sent to the server. Aggregated model 
   improves for everyone.
   
   Why this matters: GDPR bulletproof. CCPA compliant. Mechanics keep 
   their pay data on their device. You get the intelligence without the 
   liability.
   
   Stack: TensorFlow Federated or PySyft. Complex but becoming production-ready.

6. ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PROOFS (ZKP)
   Cryptographic proof that a mechanic worked at a shop without revealing 
   their identity, dates, or any other details. Shop verifies employment 
   without seeing the mechanic's profile.
   
   Why this matters: Ultimate privacy. Ultimate trust. No database of 
   employment history to hack.
   
   Stack: zk-SNARKs or zk-STARKs. Overkill for MVP but worth watching.

EARLY ADOPTERS (12-24 month horizon)
-------------------------------------

7. SPATIAL COMPUTING (Apple Vision Pro / Meta Quest 3)
   Virtual shop tours before accepting a job offer. Mechanics inspect bays, 
   tool organization, cleanliness in immersive 3D. No more "the photos were 
   from 2019" surprises.
   
   Stack: Unity or native VisionOS development. 360-degree camera rigs 
   for shops to capture tours.

8. BLOCKCHAIN CERTIFICATION VERIFICATION
   Partner with ASE, FAA, OEMs to issue NFT-based or verifiable credential 
   certifications. Mechanics own their credentials. Shops verify instantly. 
   No more fake ASE certs. Portable across platforms.
   
   Reality check: The market is not ready for this. Credentialing bodies 
   are bureaucratic. But start the conversation now. Be first when they 
   are ready.
