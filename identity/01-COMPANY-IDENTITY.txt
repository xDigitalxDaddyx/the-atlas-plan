MISSION STATEMENT (formal — for partners, press, paperwork)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Sawka Solutions exists to give mechanics — in every trade, from diesel to
  aviation to HVAC — the pay transparency and shop intelligence they have
  never had. We build tools that turn word-of-mouth into verified data, so no
  technician ever has to burn a bridge to find out a shop wasn't worth
  walking into.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MISSION (shop-floor version — for the community)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Mechanics bounce shop to shop chasing pay or culture, and find out the new
  place is worse AFTER they've burned the bridge. There's no Glassdoor for
  the trades. Word of mouth is slow and local. Tool debt makes a bad move
  devastating. We're fixing that. Real pay. Real reviews. Real shops. Built
  by a guy who's been under the lift.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPANY GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Year 1: Become the trusted source of pay and culture data in the diesel/
  heavy-duty community — 10,000 mechanics, 100 paying shops.
  Long term: The Bloomberg Terminal of the trades. Every mechanic checks
  ShopWorth before accepting an offer. Every shop checks ShopWorth before posting
  one. Every wage conversation in the industry starts with our data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE ARE BUILDING (the one-paragraph answer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ShopWorth is a transparent job board and shop review platform for ALL
  mechanics — automotive, diesel and heavy equipment, aircraft, marine,
  motorcycle, industrial, HVAC, and every other trade where someone turns
  wrenches for a living. Job listings with real pay ranges and pay structure
  (flat rate, hourly, salary — stated up front). Shop reviews from anonymous
  and verified techs, scored on the things that actually make people stay or
  quit: pay fairness, management, tool policy, work-life balance. A free True
  Pay Calculator that shows what a job REALLY pays after tool costs, slow
  weeks, insurance, and commute. Free for mechanics, forever. Shops pay to
  post jobs and manage their reputation — never to change it.
