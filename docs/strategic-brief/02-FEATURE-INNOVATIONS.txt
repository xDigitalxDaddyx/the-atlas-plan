================================================================================
Atlas STRATEGIC BRIEF — 02: FEATURE INNOVATIONS
The unfair advantages that make Atlas uncopyable.
================================================================================

P0 — BUILD THESE FIRST (High impact, do or die)
------------------------------------------------

1. PAY TRANSPARENCY LAYER
   Not just ranges — show ACTUAL pay. Mechanics upload pay stubs (anonymized 
   via OCR). You aggregate into percentiles by trade, certification, and zip 
   code. Display as: "25th: $22/hr | 50th: $28/hr | 75th: $35/hr | 90th: $42/hr"
   
   This is your nuclear option. Shops will hate it. Mechanics will worship it.
   It is the single reason they will open your app every day.
   
   Tech: Tesseract.js or AWS Textract for OCR. Stripe-style anonymization 
   pipeline. Aggregate only, never show individual data.
   
   Legal: Wage disclosure laws vary by state. Consult counsel. Consider 
   Plaid integration for bank-verified income (more trust, less fraud).

2. SOS NETWORK (Emergency Tool Lending)
   Broke your 1/2" torque wrench on a Friday afternoon? Open the app, 
   see who within 5 miles has one. Reputation-based lending (like Airbnb 
   for tools). Gamified: borrowers earn "Good Neighbor" badges. Lenders 
   earn priority access to job postings.
   
   Why this wins: No competitor has community stickiness. Job boards are 
   transactional. This makes Atlas a daily utility, not a monthly visit.

3. SHOP DNA PROFILES
   Every shop gets a generated "DNA" profile based on 50+ data points:
     - Average employee tenure
     - Turnover rate (derived from review patterns)
     - Tool budget per bay
     - Certification requirements
     - Union status
     - Flat-rate vs. hourly mix
     - Overtime frequency
     - Safety incident rate
     - Training investment per employee
   
   Updated in real-time. Visualized as a "Shop Health" score (0-100).
   Shops can see their own DNA. Mechanics use it to filter and compare.

P1 — BUILD THESE NEXT (Major differentiators)
----------------------------------------------

4. CULTURE SCORE ALGORITHM
   Kill the 1-5 star laziness. Build a multi-vector culture index:
     - Work-life balance
     - Tool allowance / reimbursement
     - Training investment
     - Safety record
     - Management quality
     - Growth opportunity
     - Overtime policy fairness
   
   Each dimension scored 1-10. Weighted by:
     - Reviewer tenure (longer = more weight)
     - Recency (last 6 months = full weight, 6-12mo = 50%, >12mo = 25%)
     - Verified status (verified = 2x weight)
   
   Visualized as an interactive radar chart on shop profiles.

5. THE BENCH (Passive Job Market)
   Mechanics set their "bench price" — the minimum pay + benefits to consider 
   leaving their current shop. They go into passive mode. Shops browse the 
   bench (anonymized profiles: "Diesel Tech, 8 years, ASE Master, $38/hr min, 
   within 25 miles of 80202"). Shops make offers. Mechanics review and accept 
   or decline.
   
   Like Tinder for trades, but with dignity. No applying. No resumes. Just 
   verified credentials and a number.
   
   Monetization: Shops pay per "bench view" or monthly subscription for 
   unlimited access. Mechanics pay $9.99/mo for premium bench placement 
   (highlighted, top of list).

6. VOICE-FIRST REVIEWS
   Mechanics work with their hands, not their thumbs. Let them dictate 
   reviews while on break. AI (Whisper API) transcribes. GPT-4 structures 
   into pros/cons. Detects sentiment. Flags concerns.
   
   Expected outcome: 3x review submission rate. Longer, richer reviews.
   Background noise filtering essential for shop environments.

P2 — BUILD THESE LATER (Nice to have, high polish)
-------------------------------------------------

7. AI SHOP INSPECTOR
   Multimodal AI (GPT-4V / Claude 3) analyzes shop photos uploaded by users.
   Detects safety violations (missing guards, cluttered bays), tool 
   organization quality, cleanliness. Auto-generates "Shop Health" reports.
   
   Gamification: Shops can request voluntary inspection. Passing earns a 
   "Certified Clean" badge. Failing gets private recommendations.

8. PREDICTIVE TURNOVER ALERTS
   ML model trained on:
     - Review sentiment shifts over time
     - Pay stagnation signals (no raises mentioned in 12+ months)
     - Seasonal patterns (turnover spikes in Q1)
     - Shop DNA degradation (scores dropping)
   
   Alert shops: "3 of your top techs are showing exit signals. Consider 
   retention conversation." Charge premium for this. $49/mo per alert tier.

9. AR TOOL VISUALIZATION
   Shop posts job requiring specific tools. Mechanic scans their toolbox with 
   AR (ARKit/ARCore). App recognizes tools via fine-tuned vision model. 
   Compares against job requirements. Highlights matches and gaps. Calculates 
   investment needed. Instant qualification check.
   
   Affiliate revenue: Links to purchase missing tools (Snap-on, Amazon, etc.).
