================================================================================
Atlas STRATEGIC BRIEF — 04: BUSINESS MODEL INNOVATIONS
How to actually make money without killing the community.
================================================================================

THE CORE PROBLEM WITH YOUR CURRENT MODEL
-----------------------------------------
Free for mechanics, paid for shops. This is a two-sided market with 
one engine. You need mechanic density BEFORE shops will pay. But you 
can't get mechanic density without shop listings. Classic chicken-and-egg.

THE FIX: B2B FIRST, B2C AS ACQUISITION
---------------------------------------
Make the shop dashboard so valuable that shops pay $99/mo before a single 
mechanic downloads the app. Then use consumer reviews and job postings 
as the acquisition channel to bring mechanics in.

REVENUE STREAMS
----------------

1. SHOP SAAS DASHBOARD (Primary revenue)
   Tier 1: $99/mo — 1-5 bays
     - Job posting (3 active)
     - Basic analytics (views, applies, culture score)
     - Review management (respond, flag, dispute)
   
   Tier 2: $199/mo — 6-15 bays
     - Unlimited job postings
     - Competitive benchmarking (salary vs. local market)
     - Predictive turnover alerts
     - The Bench access (browse passive candidates)
   
   Tier 3: $499/mo — 16+ bays / fleets
     - White-glove onboarding
     - Custom integrations (ADP, Paychex)
     - API access
     - Dedicated account manager

2. MECHANIC PREMIUM (Secondary revenue)
   Free tier: Job search, read reviews, basic profile
   
   Premium: $9.99/mo
     - The Bench (passive job mode, highlighted profile)
     - Tool Inventory OS (unlimited tools, depreciation tracking)
     - Salary negotiation agent (AI-assisted)
     - Priority support
   
   Why mechanics will pay: If The Bench gets them one job offer $2/hr 
   higher, it pays for itself in a single day.

3. TOOL FINANCING PARTNERSHIPS (Affiliate revenue)
   Partner with Snap-on, Matco, Mac Tools, Cornwell. In-app financing 
   for required tools. Revenue share on every financed tool.
   
   Example: Shop posts job requiring $3,000 in specialty tools. Mechanic 
   sees gap, clicks "Finance through Snap-on," gets 0% for 12 months. 
   You take 3-5% of the sale.
   
   Projected: $500K GMV Year 1, $5M GMV Year 3.

4. SHOP INSURANCE (Data monetization)
   Use review data and Shop DNA to offer tailored liability and workers 
   comp insurance. Shops with high culture scores get lower premiums. 
   You take a broker fee.
   
   Partner with: NEXT Insurance, Hiscox, or a trade-specific underwriter.

5. REVERSE AUCTIONS (Transaction revenue)
   Shops bid for mechanics, not the other way around. Mechanics set 
   minimums. Shops compete. You take 5% of the first-year salary 
   differential (only if the mechanic gets a raise through your platform).
   
   Ethical safeguard: Cap at $500 per placement. Mechanics keep 100% 
   of their salary.

6. UNION INTEGRATION (Institutional revenue)
   Become the official app of IUEC, IAMAW, UAW, SMART. Dues-paying 
   members get premium features free. Unions pay you a per-member fee 
   ($1-2/mo) for white-label access.
   
   Math: 2.1M union mechanics x $1.50/mo = $3.15M/mo = $37.8M ARR.
   This is the whale.

WHAT NOT TO DO
---------------
- Don't put reviews behind a paywall. That's how you die.
- Don't charge mechanics to apply for jobs. That's how you die faster.
- Don't sell mechanic data to shops without explicit consent. That's 
  how you get sued and lose the community forever.
- Don't do display ads. It cheapens the platform. Sponsored job postings 
  are fine if clearly labeled.
