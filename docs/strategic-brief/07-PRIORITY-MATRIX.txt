================================================================================
ShopWorth STRATEGIC BRIEF — 07: PRIORITY MATRIX
Impact vs. Effort. This is how we decide what to build first.
================================================================================

HIGH IMPACT / LOW EFFORT — QUICK WINS
--------------------------------------
These pay for themselves fast. Do them in Month 1.

1. Pay Stub OCR (P0)
   Effort: Low (Tesseract.js or AWS Textract)
   Impact: Nuclear. This is the reason mechanics open the app.
   Risk: Legal (wage disclosure laws vary by state)
   Success metric: 500+ verified stubs in first 30 days

2. Voice-First Reviews (P0)
   Effort: Low (Whisper API integration)
   Impact: 3x review submission rate
   Risk: Privacy (recording in shops), accuracy (background noise)
   Success metric: 3x review volume, 85% transcription accuracy

3. Dark Mode Polish / Shop Floor Aesthetic (P0)
   Effort: Low (CSS overhaul)
   Impact: Brand differentiation. Makes the app feel like it belongs 
   in a mechanic's pocket, not a banker's.
   Risk: None
   Success metric: User feedback scores improve 20%

HIGH IMPACT / MEDIUM EFFORT — MAJOR BETS
-----------------------------------------
These define the product. Do them in Months 2-4.

4. Culture Score Algorithm (P1)
   Effort: Medium (NLP pipeline, radar chart UI)
   Impact: Replaces lazy 1-5 stars with real intelligence
   Risk: Gaming (shops incentivizing positive reviews)
   Success metric: 80% of reviews include culture tags

5. Tool Inventory OS (P1)
   Effort: Medium (receipt OCR, depreciation engine, marketplace)
   Impact: Daily utility. Stickiness no competitor has.
   Risk: Complexity (tool taxonomies are massive)
   Success metric: 10,000+ tool inventories created in 6 months

6. Tri-State Review System (P1)
   Effort: Medium (verification pipeline, badge system, weighting algo)
   Impact: Trust architecture that Glassdoor never built
   Risk: Fraud (fake verification documents)
   Success metric: 40% of reviews are verified within 3 months

HIGH IMPACT / HIGH EFFORT — MOONSHOTS
--------------------------------------
These are your moat. Do them in Months 6-12.

7. Union API Integration (P0)
   Effort: High (bureaucratic partnerships, API development)
   Impact: Instant credibility. 2.1M verified users. Institutional revenue.
   Risk: Unions move slow. Politics.
   Success metric: 3 partnerships in 6 months, 50K verified members

8. The Bench (Passive Job Market) (P2)
   Effort: High (matching algorithm, offer negotiation, anonymity)
   Impact: Complete disruption of how mechanics find jobs
   Risk: Low mechanic adoption if not enough shop demand
   Success metric: 1,000 mechanics on The Bench within 6 months

9. Predictive Turnover Alerts (P2)
   Effort: High (ML model, data pipeline, shop dashboard)
   Impact: Premium revenue. Shop retention gold.
   Risk: Accuracy (false positives damage credibility)
   Success metric: 60% accuracy on turnover predictions

MEDIUM IMPACT / LOW EFFORT — FILL-INS
--------------------------------------
Do these when you have downtime or a junior dev needs a project.

10. Custom Maps Layer
    Effort: Low (Google Maps API + overlay data)
    Impact: Nice UX improvement
    Risk: None
    Success metric: 30% of users use map view

11. Review Escrow (48-hour hold)
    Effort: Low (queue system, notification pipeline)
    Impact: Reduces shop retaliation, improves shop relations
    Risk: Mechanics may perceive as censorship
    Success metric: 20% reduction in disputed reviews

MEDIUM IMPACT / MEDIUM EFFORT — BALANCED
-----------------------------------------
Build these in Phase 2 if resources allow.

12. AI Shop Inspector
    Effort: Medium (multimodal AI, photo upload, report generation)
    Impact: Differentiator. Safety focus builds trust.
    Risk: False positives on safety violations
    Success metric: 500 inspections in first 3 months

13. AR Tool Visualization
    Effort: Medium (ARKit/ARCore, vision model)
    Impact: Cool factor. Tool affiliate revenue.
    Risk: Hardware requirements limit adoption
    Success metric: 70% recognition accuracy

LOW IMPACT / ANY EFFORT — TRAPS
--------------------------------
Do not build these. They are time sinks.

14. Blockchain Certification Verification
    Effort: High
    Impact: Low (market not ready, credentialing bodies are slow)
    Why skip: Premature. Start conversations but don't build yet.

15. Custom Maps (replacing Google Maps entirely)
    Effort: High
    Impact: Low (Google Maps is fine, commodity feature)
    Why skip: Your energy is better spent on pay transparency.

16. Social Features (friend lists, messaging)
    Effort: Medium
    Impact: Low (mechanics don't want another Facebook)
    Why skip: Focus on utility, not social. The SOS Network is the 
    only social feature that matters.
