================================================================================
Atlas STRATEGIC BRIEF — 01: THE DIAGNOSIS
What is broken, missing, or dangerously assumed in the current build.
================================================================================

CRITICAL GAPS (Fix these or the product dies)
----------------------------------------------

1. THE TRUST PARADOX
   Problem: Anonymous reviews + verified reviews in the same feed creates 
   cognitive dissonance. Users won't know which weight to assign. Worse: shop 
   owners will game the verified system by forcing employees to leave 5-stars.
   
   Fix: Tri-state trust architecture
     - Anonymous (anyone can post, lowest weight)
     - Verified-Employee (proven current/former employee, highest weight)
     - Verified-Customer (proven service customer, medium weight)
   
   Why this matters: Glassdoor failed because anonymous reviews became noise.
   You have a chance to do it right from day one.

2. FREEMIUM DEATH SPIRAL
   Problem: Free for mechanics, paid for shops. This only works if you have 
   MASSIVE mechanic adoption first. Shops won't pay for a ghost town. You're 
   building a two-sided market with one engine.
   
   Fix: B2B SaaS for shops FIRST. Make the shop dashboard so valuable 
   (predictive turnover, culture benchmarking, competitive salary intel) that 
   shops pay $99/mo before a single mechanic downloads the app. Consumer reviews 
   become your acquisition channel, not your monetization engine.
   
   Revenue model pivot:
     - Shops: $99-299/mo SaaS (tiered by bay count)
     - Mechanics: Free forever (data is the product)
     - Premium mechanic tier: $9.99/mo for "The Bench" passive job mode

3. FIREBASE PHONE AUTH ONLY
   Problem: Phone numbers change. Shops have landlines. Mechanics switch 
   carriers. You need identity that persists beyond a SIM card.
   
   Fix: Move to Passkeys / WebAuthn. Biometric login. No SMS interception. 
   True persistent identity. OR partner with trade unions for verifiable 
   credential integration (IUEC, IAMAW, etc.).

4. NO UNION INTEGRATION
   Problem: 2.1 million US mechanics are union members. They have verified 
   credentials, wage scales, training records. You are ignoring the most 
   credential-rich segment of your market.
   
   Fix: Partner with one union local as a pilot. Read-only API access to 
   member verification. Instant credibility. Instant trust. Instant moat.

MEDIUM GAPS (Fix these to win)
-------------------------------

5. TRADE SILOS
   Problem: Automotive, diesel, aircraft, marine, motorcycle, industrial, HVAC 
   — these communities don't mingle. A diesel tech doesn't care about aircraft 
   MRO culture. One feed dilutes relevance for everyone.
   
   Fix: Sub-reddit style trade channels. Default feed is user's primary trade 
   only. Cross-pollination rewards: "See what's happening in Marine" card. 
   Separate culture scores, salary benchmarks, and job feeds per trade.

6. TOOL CALCULATOR ISOLATION
   Problem: Buried on shop profiles as a nice-to-have. Doesn't compound. 
   Doesn't connect to purchase history or depreciation. No one will use it 
   twice.
   
   Fix: Upgrade to Tool Inventory OS
     - Receipt OCR (Snap-on, Matco, Mac, Cornwell)
     - Depreciation curves by brand and tool type
     - Warranty expiration alerts
     - Resale marketplace integration (eBay, Facebook Marketplace)
     - Insurance valuation reports
     - Required-tool gap analysis when viewing job postings

7. GOOGLE MAPS IS A COMMODITY
   Problem: Every app has maps. You need spatial intelligence, not just 
   location pins.
   
   Fix: Layered map with
     - Real-time tool truck locations (Snap-on, Matco routes)
     - Union halls and meeting times
     - Training centers and certification events
     - Shop density heatmap (where are the jobs?)
     - Salary heatmap by zip code

LOW GAPS (Nice to have, not urgent)
------------------------------------

8. DARK THEME IS FINE BUT NOT DISTINCTIVE
   Problem: Gray/black/orange is safe. Every "edgy" app looks like this now.
   
   Fix: Lean into the shop floor aesthetic harder. Grease stains as texture 
   overlays. Warning label typography. OSHA color coding (yellow/black for 
   alerts, red for critical). Make it feel like a Snap-on catalog, not a 
   fintech app.

9. NO MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS
   Problem: Mechanics have one of the highest suicide rates of any trade. 
   Burnout is endemic. Your app will surface this in reviews. You have a 
   responsibility.
   
   Fix: AI sentiment analysis flags burnout/depression language in reviews. 
   Auto-offer resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, union EAP programs). 
   Do not sell this feature. Just be human.
