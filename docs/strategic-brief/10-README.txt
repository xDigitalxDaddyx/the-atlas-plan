HOW TO USE
-----------
1. Start with 01-DIAGNOSIS.txt. Fix the critical gaps before building 
   anything new.

2. Read 02-FEATURE-INNOVATIONS.txt and 03-TECH-BREAKTHROUGHS.txt 
   together. The features are WHAT to build. The tech is HOW to build it.

3. Use 07-PRIORITY-MATRIX.txt to decide what to build first. Don't 
   build low-impact items until high-impact items are proven.

4. Reference 04-BUSINESS-MODEL.txt when investors or stakeholders ask 
   "how do you make money?"

5. Keep 05-TRUST-SAFETY.txt open during every product meeting. Trust 
   is your only asset.

6. Review 08-ROADMAP.txt monthly. Track against kill criteria. Be 
   willing to pivot.

7. Read 09-FINAL-THOUGHTS.txt when you lose sight of why you're doing 
   this. It's the North Star.

KEY TAKEAWAYS (TL;DR)
----------------------
- Your current freemium model is backwards. B2B SaaS for shops FIRST.
- Pay transparency (real pay stubs, not ranges) is your nuclear option.
- Union integration is your moat. 2.1M verified users instantly.
- Tri-state reviews (Anonymous / Verified-Employee / Verified-Customer) 
  fix the trust paradox.
- The Bench (passive job market) disrupts how mechanics find work.
- SOS Network (tool lending) makes ShopWorth a daily utility, not a 
  monthly visit.
- If you prioritize shop revenue over mechanic trust, you will die.
