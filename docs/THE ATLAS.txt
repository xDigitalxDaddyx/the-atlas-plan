SECTION 1 — THE PRODUCT IN ONE PARAGRAPH
================================================================================
Atlas is a transparent job platform and shop review app for ALL mechanic
trades (automotive, diesel/heavy, aircraft/A&P, marine, motorcycle, bicycle,
industrial, HVAC, small engine, railroad, ag equipment, elevator). Mechanics
get real pay data, verified shop reviews, culture scores, and a True Pay
Calculator — free forever. Shops pay for job postings, featured placement,
and analytics. The wedge into the market is a standalone, free, shareable
True Pay Calculator ("Run The Numbers") seeded into the diesel community,
starting with the founder's own multi-year home community: the Facebook
group DIESEL KNUCKLEBUSTERS.

Tagline: "Know what you're walking into before you walk out."
Secondary: "It's got Atlas for a reason."

THE REAL PRODUCT (from the brainstorm, still true):
You are not building a job board. You are building LEVERAGE for mechanics.
Data is leverage. Transparency is leverage. Community is leverage. The app
is the interface. Never lose sight of this.

SECTION 2 — STRATEGIC CONFLICTS, RESOLVED (READ THIS FIRST)
================================================================================
The three source documents disagreed in three places. These are the
rulings. They are final for planning purposes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFLICT 1: WHO FIRST — SHOPS OR MECHANICS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source A (brainstorm) said: B2B SaaS for shops FIRST, because "shops won't
pay for a ghost town" and the freemium chicken-and-egg problem is fatal.
Source C (original brief) said: free for mechanics, shops pay per post.

NEW INFORMATION THAT CHANGES THE ANSWER:
The founder is a multi-year, trusted member of DIESEL KNUCKLEBUSTERS with
multiple community-viral posts and field-proven credibility (profile photo:
balancing on a CAT637G tractor transmission hanging from a crane, one hand
wrenching). This is a warm, dense, real-time pool of actual working diesel
mechanics — the exact cold-start asset the shop-first pivot was designed to
compensate for. The brainstorm made its shop-first recommendation WITHOUT
knowing this pool existed.

RULING — TWO-TRACK, MECHANIC-DENSITY-FIRST:
- ACQUISITION is mechanic-first. The diesel community (DKB + adjacent
  diesel/heavy groups) is the beachhead. The calculator is the wedge.
  Mechanic trust and review density come BEFORE any shop sales motion.
- REVENUE is still B2B. Mechanics never pay. Shops pay once there is
  demonstrable mechanic density to recruit from. The shop dashboard and
  paid tiers are built early but not SOLD until density milestones hit
  (see Section 8 metrics).
- Why this works when the brainstorm feared it wouldn't: the brainstorm
  assumed you'd have to BUY mechanic attention. You don't. You already
  HAVE it. The chicken-and-egg problem is solved by the founder's
  standing in the community — but only for diesel. Every other trade and
  metro must be seeded deliberately (see Section 8).
- What survives from the shop-first argument intact: shops remain the
  ONLY revenue source, the dashboard must be genuinely valuable (not a
  ghost-town upsell), and the pitch "one filled bay pays for a decade of
  subscription" ($20-25K/mo cost of an empty bay; $50-100K to replace an
  A-level tech) remains the core sales math.

SECTION 3 — NON-NEGOTIABLES (GLOBAL CONSTRAINTS FOR EVERY AGENT)
================================================================================
These override everything. Any agent output that violates these is rejected.

1. NEVER sell review removal, review suppression, or reputation laundering.
   Monetization = visibility and tools, never silence. Say this publicly
   and often — it is a trust asset. (Sources A + B both flag this as fatal.)

2. MECHANICS NEVER PAY. Free forever: jobs, reviews, calculator, pay data.

3. TWO LANGUAGES, NEVER MIXED ON ONE SCREEN (Source C branding guide):
   - mechanic_copy: real talk, toolbox-at-lunch voice. Direct, honest,
     no corporate BS. Approved examples live in 06-BRANDING-VOICE.txt.
   - shop_copy: professional, respectful, data-driven. Same honesty,
     different wrapper.
   - neutral_copy: shared screens (login, settings) — clean and minimal.
   Every string tagged by audience in code. UI is ALWAYS clean and
   professional; the voice lives in microcopy. Never a slang shitfest.

4. MESSAGING RULES THAT WILL GET YOU FLAMED IF BROKEN (Source B):
   - Never say "technician shortage" in mechanic-facing copy — it reads as
     owner propaganda. Say "shops competing for you."
   - No stock photos of clean models holding wrenches. Instant death.
   - No HR-speak ("competitive salary," "fast-paced environment").
     Mock it if anything — it's the exact language mechanics distrust.
   - Name flat rate out loud. The community rewards anyone who says it.
   - Anonymity is sacred — employer retaliation fear is real and constant.

5. MENTAL HEALTH IMPERATIVE (Source A): mechanics have one of the highest
   suicide rates of any trade. When the review pipeline detects burnout/
   crisis language, auto-offer resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline,
   union EAP). Never sell this feature. Never use this data for targeting.
   This is a moral obligation, not a feature.

6. DESIGN SYSTEM TOKENS (Source C, locked):
   Background #1A1A1A · Surface #2A2A2A · Primary #FF6B00 (warning orange)
   Primary Light #FF8C42 · Secondary #4A90D9 · Success #4CAF50
   Danger #E53935 · Text #FFFFFF / #B0B0B0 · Border #3A3A3A
   Mobile-first, one-handed use, bold condensed typography, shop-floor
   aesthetic. Feel like a Snap-on catalog, not a fintech app. The
   calculator uses the same tokens (#ff6b00 accent) so the brand reads
   as one product family from first contact.

7. PRIVACY: pay stub uploads are anonymized (blur everything but the rate),
   aggregate-only display, never show individual data. No selling mechanic
   data to shops without explicit consent. Ever.

SECTION 4 — THE WEDGE: CALCULATOR + DIESEL KNUCKLEBUSTERS PLAYBOOK
================================================================================
This is the single most important section for launch. The calculator is not
a feature — it is the "IN." It is a content engine disguised as a tool, and
it passes community mod review where "download my app" posts get deleted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 THE TOOL: "RUN THE NUMBERS — THE MECHANIC'S TRUE PAY CALCULATOR"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Build spec: calculator_prompt.txt (complete, unchanged — hand it verbatim
to the build agent). Summary of what it must be:
  - Standalone single-file web page, no backend, no login, works over
    file:// or any static host. Buildable in ONE agent session.
  - Compares two job offers side by side: flat rate / hourly / salary,
    flagged vs physical hours, slow-week modeling, insurance, 401k match,
    PTO, tool allowance, tool costs, commute at IRS mileage rate.
  - Headline output: EFFECTIVE HOURLY WAGE per job + delta + winner
    banner + plain-English verdict ("The $5/hr raise is real — but only
    in the busy months.").
  - SHARE BUTTON rendering a screenshot-friendly summary card sized for
    a Facebook post. THIS IS THE GROWTH LOOP. The share card carries
    Atlas branding (#ff6b00 on dark) — every screenshot posted anywhere
    is an ad.
  - Validation test vector (must pass): flat rate $32/hr, flags 40 normal,
    20 slow weeks at 25 hrs, 50 physical hrs/wk, $1,350/mo tool payment,
    no benefits, 30-mi commute — outputs must be sane and consistent.

BRIDGE TO THE APP (new, not in original spec — add after core works):
  - Footer line on the share card: "Atlas — know what you're walking into"
  - Optional (no-pressure) waitlist capture: one field, phone or email,
    copy: "We're building the review board the trade actually needs.
    Get in early." Never gate the calculator behind it. Gating kills sharing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 THE CHANNEL: DIESEL KNUCKLEBUSTERS (FOUNDER'S HOME FIELD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Founder assets that cannot be bought:
  - Multi-year membership, multiple community-viral posts
  - Profile photo IS the credibility: balancing on a CAT637G tractor
    transmission hanging from a crane, one-handed wrenching. This reads
    instantly to every tech in the group: "this guy has been there."
  - This is the primary testing pool. Treat it accordingly.

THE SEQUENCE (do not deviate):
  STEP 1 — MODS FIRST. Private message to group admins/moderators BEFORE
    posting anything. Not a pitch — a respect play: "Long-time member,
    built a free tool that settles the flat-rate vs hourly arguments we
    have in here every week. Want to make sure it's cool to share, and
    happy to give you guys first look / lifetime status when the bigger
    thing launches." Mods who feel consulted become allies; mods who feel
    bypassed become executioners.
  STEP 2 — SHARE THE CALCULATOR AS A MEMBER, NOT A FOUNDER. First post is
    a genuine contribution: run a scenario the group argues about (flat
    rate $32 vs hourly $28 + benefits + tool allowance), post the share
    card, ask what the group thinks of the math. Let members break it.
    Fix what they find. THAT is the beta test.
  STEP 3 — RECRUIT FOUNDING REVIEWERS FROM THE COMMENTS. The mechanics who
    engage hardest are your founding 50. Personal outreach: founding-member
    flair, lifetime free everything, their names (if they want) in the
    credits. 50 honest reviews in one trade > 5,000 scattered.
  STEP 4 — ONLY AFTER DENSITY: the app reveal. By then it's not a stranger
    promoting an app — it's a known member showing the group what their
    feedback built. "You guys broke the calculator, so I built the rest."

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT (from Source B, adapted to Facebook):
  - Value-first, link-second. Always.
  - Never astroturf. Communities detect and destroy it, and the founder's
    multi-year reputation is worth more than any growth hack.
  - Dual-sided awareness: shop owners lurk in diesel groups too. The same
    calculator content that recruits mechanics is quietly demoing the
    platform to future paying shops. Don't pander to either side — the
    transparency IS the pitch to both.
  - Everything in the group's voice. The founder already speaks it. No
    marketing copy in group posts — write like the viral posts that
    already worked.

EXPANSION CHANNELS (only after DKB works — Source B playbook, in order):
  1. Adjacent diesel/heavy Facebook groups + r/Justrolledintotheshop,
     r/Truckers (value-first, rule-compliant; calculators pass mod review)
  2. TikTok/YouTube Shorts: "Run the Numbers" calculator demos,
     "Shop Confessions" (anonymized real reviews over shop b-roll),
     "Pay Range or It Didn't Happen" — 20-30 banked before app launch
  3. Trade schools: UTI, WyoTech, Lincoln Tech diesel programs — free
     pizza + "know your worth" + calculator demo. Cheap, fast installs.
  4. Tool trucks: Snap-on/Mac/Matco dealers are franchisees — local deals,
     sticker/card on the truck. Reaches 100% of working techs in a metro.
  5. Mid-tier trade creators (50K-500K followers, e.g. Flat Rate Master
     tier and below) — audiences are 90%+ working techs, rates far below
     consumer influencers.

SECTION 5 — TARGET ARCHITECTURE (RECONCILED)
================================================================================
Stack (from Source C, unchanged):
  Frontend:  React Native (Expo recommended for iteration speed)
  Backend:   Node.js + Express, PostgreSQL, JWT + Firebase Auth (phone)
  Hosting:   AWS/Vercel/Railway — cheap to start
  Payments:  Stripe · Maps: Google Maps API
  PLUS (from Source A, scheduled): Whisper API (voice reviews), OCR
  (Tesseract.js or AWS Textract) for pay stubs, GPT-class structuring.

DATA MODEL DELTAS — the Source C schema is the base; the brainstorm's
diagnosis requires these changes in migration v2 (build them NOW, not
later — retrofitting trust architecture is how platforms die):

  reviews table:
    + verification_state  ENUM ('anonymous','verified_employee',
      'verified_customer')  DEFAULT 'anonymous'
      — replaces the single 'verified' boolean (keep boolean as computed
        shorthand: verified = state != 'anonymous')
    + verification_method VARCHAR (pay_stub | w2 | union | receipt | none)
    + review_weight       DECIMAL — computed from state x tenure x recency
    + culture dimension ratings already exist (4 sliders: pay structure,
      management, tool policy, work/life) — extend to 7 when the Culture
      Score ships (add: safety, growth, overtime fairness) but DO NOT
      block launch on it.
  shops table:
    + pay_structure badge field EXISTS (pay_structure enum) — surface it
      prominently in UI ("Pay Structure" badge is a differentiator no
      general job board has).
    + wheels_certified    BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE (Phase 2: posts real pay
      ranges + >=3.5 stars with 5+ reviews + responds to reviews)
  NEW table: pay_stubs (OCR pipeline)
    id, user_id (hashed), shop_id, trade_type, zip_code,
    extracted_rate DECIMAL, confidence DECIMAL, status ENUM
    ('pending','verified','rejected'), created_at
    — raw image stored encrypted, PII blurred, never exposed via API.
      Percentile aggregation view: 25th/50th/75th/90th by trade+zip.
  NEW table: waitlist (from calculator bridge)
    contact VARCHAR, source VARCHAR DEFAULT 'calculator', trade_type,
    zip_code, created_at — this table doubles as the metro-selection
    dataset (Section 2, Conflict 3 ruling).

AUTH: ship Firebase phone auth per Source C for MVP. Passkeys/WebAuthn
(Source A) is the identity endgame — pilot in Phase 2, do not block launch.

FEATURE PRIORITIES (Source A priority matrix, compressed to what survives
contact with the launch plan):
  BUILD NOW (Phase 1): calculator (wedge), tri-state review architecture,
    pay-stub OCR pipeline, pay percentile display, pay structure badges,
    core app (jobs/reviews/profiles per Source C spec).
  BUILD NEXT (Phase 2): Culture Score radar, voice-first reviews (Whisper),
    shop dashboard + Stripe billing, Atlas Certified Shop + bay sticker,
    review velocity detection, 48-hour review escrow.
  BUILD LATER (Phase 3): The Bench, Tool Inventory OS, SOS Network,
    predictive turnover, union API pilot.
  NEVER / TRAPS (Source A, confirmed): blockchain cert verification,
    replacing Google Maps, friend-list/messaging social features.
    The SOS Network is the only social feature that matters — and not yet.

  - Reviews < 10/week by week 6 -> ambassador/founding program isn't
    working; pivot incentives BEFORE spending on ads.
  - Shop conversion < 5% of claimed profiles by week 12 -> reprice or
    pull the pay-per-application option forward.

  - <100 DAU by month 3 · <10 paying shops by month 6 · <1,000 verified
    reviews by month 6 · union pilot unstarted by month 6 (long-lead).

The mechanic trade is one of the last industries where workers have no
leverage. They don't know what they're worth, what other shops pay, or
which shops are toxic until it's too late.

Atlas changes that — not by being a better job board, but by treating
mechanics like professionals with choices. If a mechanic gets a $5/hr
raise because of your pay data, he tells every mechanic he knows. Word of
mouth in the trades is everything. One success story is worth 1,000 ads.
Make success stories the marketing strategy.

The founder is already standing in the community this product serves —
literally, in the profile picture, one hand on a wrench over a hanging
CAT637G transmission. That is the whole brand. Build it honest, build it
for them, and they will build the empire.

Year 1: 10,000 mechanics, 100 paying shops.
Year 3: the Bloomberg Terminal of the trades.
Everything else is just code.
