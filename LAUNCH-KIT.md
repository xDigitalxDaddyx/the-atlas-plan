## 4. Launch-day playbook

1. **Post the image, not just a link.** `share-card.png` inline; put the hosted link in the
   body or first comment ("link in comments" often beats Facebook's link demotion).
2. **Timing.** Weekday evenings (~6–9pm local) or weekend mornings — mechanics scroll after work.
3. **Reply fast.** Answer every comment in the first hour. Early engagement pushes it into more feeds.
4. **"Post your number" is the engine.** Every screenshot is a share card carrying the © line.
5. **Don't reveal the crash or unemployment.** Frame it as "my last two jobs," never "I'm between jobs."
6. **Don't mention the future app.** Pure free tool. Zero sales energy.
7. **Don't blast 10 groups at once.** Space posts across days; vary wording (use the 4 scripts above).
8. **Track.** Use a free link shortener with a different link per group (bit.ly / s.co) to see which
   group drives clicks, or just count "I ran it" comments.
9. **Watch for "what tool is this?"** — reply with the link every time. That's the growth loop.
