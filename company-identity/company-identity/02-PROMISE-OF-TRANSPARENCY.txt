================================================================================
THE TRANSPARENCY PROMISE
Public-facing. This can be posted verbatim. It is a commitment, not marketing.
================================================================================

THE PROMISE (community version — postable as-is):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Here's the deal, in plain language, because the whole point of this thing is
that somebody finally says it plain:

1. MECHANICS NEVER PAY. Not for jobs, not for reviews, not for the
   calculator, not ever. The people turning the wrenches are not the product
   being sold.

2. NO SHOP CAN PAY TO HIDE, CHANGE, OR DELETE A REVIEW. Not for any amount
   of money. Shops can respond publicly — that's it. If a shop ever offers
   money to make a bad review disappear, the answer is no, and the fact that
   they asked becomes part of their story.

3. YOUR IDENTITY IS LOCKED DOWN. Anonymous means anonymous — no manager
   sees a name. Verified reviews prove you worked there without exposing who
   you are. Pay stubs get blurred down to the number. Retaliation is the
   whole reason this platform needs to exist, so protecting reviewers is not
   a feature — it's the foundation.

4. THE MATH IS OPEN. Pay ranges, culture scores, and would-return
   percentages come from real reviews and real pay data — the methodology is
   published, not a black box. If the numbers look wrong, you can check how
   they were made.

5. THE BUSINESS MODEL IS PUBLIC. Shops pay to post jobs, feature their
   profile, and see analytics. That is the entire revenue model. No selling
   your data. No ads cluttering your feed. If that ever changes, it changes
   in public, announced, before it happens.

6. IF WE GET IT WRONG, SAY SO. A platform built on honesty about shops has
   to take honesty about itself. Call it out. It gets answered.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION (for partners, press, group moderators):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Wheels is built on a single non-negotiable: transparency cannot be
  purchased. Mechanics never pay. Shops pay for visibility and tools —
  job posts, featured placement, analytics — and can respond to reviews
  publicly, but no amount of money changes, hides, or removes a review.
  Reviewer identity is protected against retaliation, pay data is
  anonymized and aggregate-only, scoring methodology is published, and
  the revenue model is disclosed in full. We hold the platform to the
  same standard it holds shops.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS PROMISE EXISTS (internal — the strategic reason):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The trades run on word of mouth because word of mouth can't be bought.
  The moment a platform's reviews are for sale, it becomes Indeed with a
  darker theme, and the community will say so publicly and permanently.
  This promise is not ethics decoration — it is the entire competitive
  moat. It is also, not coincidentally, the right thing to do.
