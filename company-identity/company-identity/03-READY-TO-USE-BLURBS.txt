THE 150-WORD (moderator outreach, design-partner shops, anyone asking
seriously)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Long-time member here, and I'm building something I want to run by you
properly. It's called Wheels — a transparent job board and shop review
platform for mechanics across all trades. The short version: techs can see
real pay ranges and pay structure (flat rate vs hourly stated up front),
read and leave reviews scored on the stuff that actually makes people quit
— pay fairness, management, tool policy, work-life balance — and use a free
calculator that shows what a job really pays after tool costs, insurance,
and slow weeks.

Free for mechanics, forever. Shops pay to post jobs — and no shop can pay
to hide or remove a review, ever. That line is the whole company.

The calculator is live and free. I'm sharing it here first because this
group is home, and I'd rather have you guys break it before the internet
does."

THE SHOP-OWNER VERSION (for design partners and early shop conversations —
professional register, same honesty)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Wheels is a hiring and reputation platform built exclusively for the
mechanical trades. Mechanics come for transparent pay data and verified shop
reviews; shops get in front of technicians who are actively comparing their
options — with real numbers, not recycled job-board copy.

One filled bay covers the subscription for a decade: an empty bay costs
$20,000–$25,000 a month, and replacing an A-level tech runs $50,000–$100,000.
Shops that post real pay ranges and respond to reviews earn a visible
credibility signal to the exact workforce they're trying to hire.

What we don't sell: review removal, suppression, or reputation laundering.
Transparency is the product — which is precisely why the technicians you
want will trust what they see about you here."
